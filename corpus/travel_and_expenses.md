# Halcyon Robotics — Travel & Expense Policy

*Policy FIN-TRV-007 · Owner: Finance*

## 1. Booking travel

All business travel must be booked through the company travel portal, Wayfarer, at least 14 days before departure where possible. Trips must be approved by the employee's line manager before booking.

Economy class is the default for all flights. Premium economy is permitted for flights longer than 6 hours. Business class requires approval from a VP and is only granted for flights longer than 10 hours.

Rail is preferred over flying for journeys shorter than 4 hours.

## 2. Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 180 EUR in most cities and 260 EUR in London, New York, Zurich and Tokyo. Stays above the limit require written approval from Finance before booking.

## 3. Per diem

Employees on business travel receive a daily meal allowance (per diem) of 55 EUR for domestic trips and 75 EUR for international trips. When a meal is provided by a client or a conference, the per diem for that day is reduced by 20 EUR per provided meal.

## 4. Mileage

Use of a private car for business travel is reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre. Parking and tolls are reimbursed against receipts.

## 5. Submitting expenses

Expenses must be submitted in the expense tool, Ledgerly, within 30 days of the purchase date. Every item above 25 EUR needs a receipt. Expenses submitted later than 90 days after purchase are not reimbursed.

Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll run after approval.

## 6. Approval limits

- Up to 500 EUR: line manager approval.
- 500 to 5,000 EUR: department head approval.
- Above 5,000 EUR: CFO approval.

## 7. Non-reimbursable items

Alcohol, traffic fines, personal entertainment (films, minibar) and upgrades not covered by this policy are not reimbursed.

## 8. Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than 6 times per year may request a corporate credit card. Corporate card statements must be reconciled in Ledgerly by the 5th of each month.
