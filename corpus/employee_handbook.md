# Halcyon Robotics — Employee Handbook

*Version 4.2 · Owner: People Operations · Applies to all full-time employees*

## 1. Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in each employee's local time zone. Outside core hours, employees may arrange their schedule freely as long as they work an average of 40 hours per week over a four-week period. Meetings must not be scheduled outside core hours without the attendee's agreement.

## 2. Probation

New employees serve a probation period of 90 calendar days. During probation either party may end employment with one week of written notice. A probation review meeting with the line manager takes place in week 10.

## 3. Annual leave

Full-time employees accrue 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing at 2 days per completed month of service. Part-time employees accrue leave pro rata to their contracted hours.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the following calendar year. Carried-over days expire if they are not used by 31 March. Leave requests of more than 10 consecutive working days must be submitted at least 30 days in advance.

The office is closed between 24 December and 1 January. These closure days are granted in addition to annual leave and are not deducted from an employee's allowance.

## 4. Sick leave

Employees must notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 5 consecutive working days.

## 5. Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken any time in the first 12 months after the birth or adoption. Employees become eligible after 6 months of continuous service.

## 6. Remote work

Halcyon is a hybrid company. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Engineering teams working on hardware must be on site on Tuesdays for integration testing. Working from another country is permitted for up to 30 days per calendar year, subject to manager approval and a tax check by People Operations.

Every employee receives a one-off home office stipend of 750 EUR in their first month.

## 7. Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 EUR for courses, conferences and books. Unused budget does not roll over. Conference travel funded from the learning budget follows the travel policy.

## 8. Performance reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in April and October. Salary adjustments are decided once a year, following the October review cycle, and take effect on 1 January.
